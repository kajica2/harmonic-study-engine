● How it works

Three moves. One screen.

You don't need a tutorial. The engine is designed to be explored — every slider, layer, and preset is one click from the canvas.

STEP 01

Upload audio

Drop an MP3, WAV, or FLAC — or hit the mic toggle to capture a live take. The engine maps frequency, rhythm, and dynamics in real time.

STEP 02

Shape the visual system

Choose a preset, layer overlays, sculpt intensity, sensitivity, bloom, and grain. Watch the canvas respond instantly as you tweak.

STEP 03

Record and share

Pick 9:16, 1:1, or 16:9, set a duration, and capture. Downloads as a .webm ready for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, or X.
